# DAILY — Sales Presentation Script

> Accompanies `DAILY_Enterprise_Commercial_Presentation_EN.pptx/.pdf` (39 slides, ~45 min) and `DAILY_Executive_Summary_EN.pdf` (5 slides, 5 min). The same notes are in the PowerPoint notes pane.

## Rules for presenting

- Screenshots are **real screens running a demo company**. Say so at the start and never present them as client results.
- **Screenshots were captured with the DAILY interface switched to English** (language selector, top bar). A small number of residual labels were translated at capture time; see DAILY_English_Translation_QA.md. Data and layout are unaltered.
- Currency on screen is the demo company's own currency, shown as $; no conversion was applied.
- Every slide carries a tag: **Available feature**, **Illustrative scenario**, **Available + projected**, **Available + in development** or **Commercial proposal**. Respect it when speaking.
- **Occupational health and safety does not exist today.** Neither does the time-and-motion study nor shift generation straight from demand.
- An inventory difference appears **when a count is recorded**. Do not say DAILY detects it on its own in real time.
- Recommendations are **explainable analytics** (rules and statistics), even if a screen name says "AI".
- **The monthly Executive Dashboard (/kpis) is being redesigned and currently shows sample values with an example brand: do not open it in a demo.** Use TODAY and the Executive Loss Report.
- The header KPIs of the Loss Prevention Center and Merchandise Control are sample values: do not quote them.
- The comparison with Walmart or Costco is about **capabilities**, not products: there is no integration or relationship with those companies.
- The financial example is **illustrative**: never promise savings.
- There are no final prices, clients or testimonials in this presentation.

## Script by slide (full version)

### 1. Cover — DAILY: operational supervision and control for retail

- **Say:** "DAILY brings into one platform what many chains keep in spreadsheets and separate systems: inventory, sales, shrink, loss prevention, staff and purchasing."
- **Show:** the three screens; they are real, running a demo company with ten branches. The interface is shown in English; DAILY runs in English or Spanish per user.
- **Benefit:** one version of the numbers for the whole operation.
- **Common question:** "Is it an ERP?" → It does not replace your ERP or POS; it adds operational supervision and control, and receives data through import or API.

### 2. What erodes margin in a retail chain today

- **Say:** walk through the cards and ask which ones the client recognizes.
- **Show:** the red card sums up the consequence.
- **Benefit:** name the problem before talking about software.
- **Ask the client:** "When do you find out about an inventory difference today: the same day or at month end?"

### 3. How DAILY connects the operation

- **Say:** "Every step of the merchandise leaves a movement in a single stock ledger. When something does not reconcile, DAILY turns it into an alert or a case with an owner."
- **Show:** follow the ring from Receiving to Corrective actions.
- **Benefit:** end-to-end traceability.
- **Common question:** "Do I need to change my POS?" → No. Sales can be recorded in the DAILY POS or loaded through import or API; the exact integration is validated during the assessment.

### 4. Ten work areas, one shared data set

- **Say:** "This is the real DAILY menu, grouped the way a chain works: plan and buy, receive and store, sell, dispatch, protect quality, control shrink, prevent loss, organize staff, report and administer."
- **Show:** the ten areas; the yellow tag marks the parts still in development.
- **Benefit:** the client sees the full scope before the detail.
- **Clarify:** what is available, in development and proposed is on the feature status slide.

### 5. TODAY: how the operation is doing, hour by hour

- **Say:** "A manager opens TODAY and sees sales, target attainment, margin, average ticket and shrink as a share of sales, with the actual curve against the hourly target and the notices that need attention."
- **Show:** (1) critical decisions for the shift; (2) KPIs against the previous period; (3) actual sales vs. target; (4) notices with direct access.
- **Benefit:** decide during the day, not at close.
- **Clarify:** the monthly Executive Dashboard is being redesigned; for the demo we use TODAY and the Executive Loss Report, which read recorded data.

### 6. Every deviation with an owner, a deadline and an impact

- **Say:** "Each alert has a severity, an owner, a time limit and an estimated impact. The right panel shows progress: detected, assigned, investigation, validation and closure."
- **Show:** the summary at the top, the list and the panel of the selected alert.
- **Benefit:** nothing is left without an owner and delays are visible.
- **Common question:** "Who receives the alerts?" → They are assigned to an owner and also appear in the mobile web app.

### 7. Every open document: which step it is in and who has it

- **Say:** "DAILY tracks every operational document —purchase order, customer order, shrink, transfer, return, adjustment, count, task— and shows its current step, the next one, the owner and the due date. At the top, the Exception Center groups what got blocked or inconsistent: a cash shift left open, shrink deducted without approval, a transfer never received."
- **Show:** the exceptions and the counters by case type.
- **Benefit:** processes stop depending on people's memory.
- **Clarify:** progress is derived from the real state of each document; it is not a manual log.

### 8. 8 phones expected; the count found 6

- **Say:** "Let's follow a case from start to finish. The system expects 8 units of a high-value phone in the store; the physical count records 6. When the count is saved, DAILY calculates the difference and its value and opens case DEMO-001."
- **Show:** the red notice on the product record: difference, value, audit interval, priority and owner.
- **Benefit:** the difference appears when counting, not at close.
- **Clarify:** demo case on fictitious data, executed with the real logic. The difference appears when the count is recorded; it is not automatic real-time tracking.

### 9. Every product movement, with document and user

- **Say:** "The owner does not search through spreadsheets: they open the product ledger. They see receipts, POS sales, transfers and adjustments, with date, document, lot, user and valued balance, plus the reconciliation for the period."
- **Show:** the period summary and the ledger lines.
- **Benefit:** the investigation starts from recorded movements.
- **Common question:** "Can movements be altered?" → The history is sealed and its integrity can be verified on demand.

### 10. Investigation record: interval, causes and recount

- **Say:** "The record bounds the audited interval between the last correct count and the current one, counts the movements in that window and evaluates causes against the evidence. It does not presume theft. It asks for an independent recount and, if confirmed, a different supervisor authorizes the adjustment."
- **Show:** the interval, the cause evaluation and the two resolution blocks.
- **Benefit:** documented causes instead of assumptions, with segregation of duties.
- **Clarify:** DAILY guides the investigation; a person decides the cause and the adjustment.

### 11. The case stays in a file until it is closed

- **Say:** "The case enters the Loss Prevention Center along with the others: product, store, zone, difference, value at risk, priority, status and owner. It can be resolved, sent to recount or escalated, and every step is kept in the case history."
- **Show:** the incidents and cases table; on the right, the progress of an alert.
- **Benefit:** nobody adjusts inventory without leaving a trail.
- **Clarify:** the header KPIs of the Loss Prevention Center still show sample values; the cases table does read the recorded cases.

### 12. From count to closure: six steps, four modules

- **Say:** "In short: the count detects, reconciliation calculates, the ledger explains, the record investigates, the supervisor authorizes and the case closes. Four modules working on the same data."
- **Show:** the sequence and the module involved in each step.
- **Benefit:** a repeatable, auditable process for every difference.
- **Ask the client:** "How many steps of this process do you do outside the system today?"

### 13. How much is lost, where and why

- **Say:** "The Shrinkage & Waste control board shows total shrink and shrink as a share of sales against target, by type, department, product and cause, plus the trend by branch."
- **Show:** KPIs, evolution against target, breakdown by type and by department.
- **Benefit:** attack the causes that cost the most.
- **Common question:** "Who approves shrink?" → Someone other than the person who records it; until then, stock is not deducted.

### 14. Approval, causes, investigation and prevention

- **Say:** "Around the board there are four tools: the approvals inbox, the cause matrix with Pareto, investigations with action plans and expiry prevention with FEFO."
- **Show:** the four screens.
- **Benefit:** shrink records turn into decisions.
- **Ask the client:** "Who approves shrink today, and how long does it take?"

### 15. What is on hand, what it is worth and what is at risk

- **Say:** "Stock & Movements shows value, units, turnover and coverage; below, items under minimum, close to expiry, out of stock and value at risk, each with a suggested action."
- **Show:** the risk cards and recent movements.
- **Benefit:** the inventory owner prioritizes with data.
- **Common question:** "Can I see it by branch?" → Yes, with filters by branch, department and category.

### 16. Locations, transfers, counts and scanning

- **Say:** "The warehouse is run by location: zone map, store-to-store transfers with verification, physical counts and scanner operations."
- **Show:** the four tools.
- **Benefit:** every movement is tied to a location and an owner.
- **Common question:** "Which scanners does it support?" → Phone camera and keyboard-mode scanners; the exact model is validated in the pilot.

### 17. High-value products, controlled unit by unit

- **Say:** "For phones and other high-value products, DAILY tracks each unit by IMEI or serial number: where it is, its status and who holds it. Units not located open an investigation. It also schedules sensitive counts with due dates and recounts."
- **Show:** the device statuses and the table with custody and last movement.
- **Benefit:** unit-level control where a single loss is costly.
- **Clarify:** cases accept CCTV references as evidence, but there is no camera integration. Some header KPIs of the Loss Prevention Center still show sample values.

### 18. Executive Loss Report: a daily snapshot for leadership

- **Say:** "Every day a consolidated loss report is produced from the same sources as Shrink and Inventory: confirmed loss, shrink as a share of sales, differences under investigation, priorities with owners and branch comparison. It states whether the cut is preliminary and which stores did not sync."
- **Show:** the KPIs, the priorities table and the sync status.
- **Benefit:** leadership gets the same number the operation sees.
- **Common question:** "Can it be exported?" → Yes, as PDF and Excel; daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.

### 19. What sells, what it earns and where the gap is

- **Say:** "Net sales, target attainment, profit, margin, transactions and ticket, against the previous period; below, the curve against target, the share by department and performance by branch."
- **Show:** KPIs, curve and departments.
- **Benefit:** see the gap to target in time.
- **Common question:** "Does it reconcile with inventory?" → Yes; the data quality block shows whether each sale has a cost and a reconciled movement.

### 20. From need to purchase order, in a 12-phase cycle

- **Say:** "The MRP calculates, product by product, net stock, daily sales, coverage, safety stock and reorder point, and suggests quantity, cost and supplier. Orders go through approval, issue, follow-up and receiving, and supplier performance is measured on lead time and fulfilment."
- **Show:** the cycle KPIs, the 12 phases and the list of needs.
- **Benefit:** buy what is needed, on time.
- **Clarify:** suggested orders require approval by a person; forecast accuracy is shown as is, to be improved with the client's data.

### 21. Shifts, coverage and labor cost on one board

- **Say:** "Schedules & Coverage compares projected sales with labor hours, measures productivity by department and labor cost against budget, and shows the weekly and monthly schedule."
- **Show:** the chart and the schedule.
- **Benefit:** spot overstaffing and coverage gaps before publishing the schedule.
- **Clarify:** the generator rules are configurable and must be validated with the client's legal team.

### 22. Attendance, tasks and demand-based planning

- **Say:** "Today DAILY records attendance, late arrivals and floor tasks, and compares projected sales with labor hours. What does not exist yet is a time-and-motion study or automatic shift generation straight from demand."
- **Show:** on the left what is available; on the right the concept.
- **Benefit:** daily follow-up of the real work.
- **Clarify:** the projected column is a concept; do not present it as a finished feature.

### 23. Act before the product expires

- **Say:** "DAILY identifies expired or soon-to-expire lots, with location and value at risk, and follows a handling path: review, consider markdown, isolate, decide disposition and log temperature."
- **Show:** lots that need attention, the matrix and the path.
- **Benefit:** less waste and better customer protection.
- **Common question:** "Does it monitor the cold chain?" → It records temperatures and excursions per cold room; automatic capture with sensors is defined per project.

### 24. From a lot about to expire to an approved shrink record

- **Say:** "Second case: a lot of milk expires. Quality flags it in the matrix; TODAY and the Alert Center show it as a notice with an owner; if it does not sell at a markdown, shrink is recorded; a different person approves it and only then is it deducted from stock; the shrink board and the executive report reflect it."
- **Show:** the four screens, left to right.
- **Benefit:** expiry is managed as a process, not a surprise.
- **Clarify:** automatic blocking of expired lots at the POS exists as a rule, but it is disabled until defined with each client.

### 25. Every store, measured with the same KPIs
